Simple 3-Step Classroom Workflow

Built for Chromebooks, laptops, tablets, and phones — 100% browser-based with no login required.

1

Choose Language & Mode

Select your target language studio, pick Independent (1 Student) or Pair Task (2 Students), enter your name(s), and choose your dialect.

2

Speak with Live Transcription

Click Start Recording to watch live audio frequency bars and automatic real-time speech transcription. In Pair Mode, press Pass Turn (or Tab) between speakers.

3

Review Feedback & Download MP3 + TXT

Listen to your recording, review instant English grammar feedback, reattempt if desired, or download both your MP3 audio and TXT report in one click.
